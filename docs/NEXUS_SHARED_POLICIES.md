# Nexus Web Technology — Shared Product Policies

This directory is the canonical policy source for the Nexus ecosystem.

Products covered:
- Nexus Plus
- Geeta Nexus
- Nexus Wave website and web services

Canonical URLs:
- Privacy Policy: /legal/privacy
- Terms and Conditions: /legal/terms
- Refund Policy: /legal/refund

The same policy text should be surfaced by the website and by the mobile apps. Product-specific screens may add a short introduction, but must not replace or contradict the canonical policy.

## Account and identity
Website, Nexus Plus and Geeta Nexus should use the same Supabase project and Supabase Auth user identity. An authenticated user's Supabase UUID is the shared account identifier.

## Team and community
- Team applications are submitted through Geeta Nexus / the Join Our Team workflow.
- Applications are stored in Supabase.
- The main administrator is the configured primary Supabase account.
- Approved team members can publish community posts only after approval.
- Spiritual posts for Geeta Nexus are free.
- Nexus Plus community content is separately permissioned.
- Teaching contributors may use both Geeta Nexus and Nexus Plus as teaching aids.

## Product identity
Nexus Web Technology is the development identity.
Spiritual work is presented under **Nexus Dharma**, a dedicated spiritual activity within the Nexus ecosystem.
